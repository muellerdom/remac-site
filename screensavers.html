Screensavers

When ReMac's home screen sits idle for five minutes, your Mac shows one of these instead of a still picture. They stay dark and keep drifting, so nothing burns in, and the first press on the remote only ends the screensaver. Pick one in ReMac Settings › General › Screensaver. Tap a design to watch it large.
